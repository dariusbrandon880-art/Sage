# SAGE Career Queue #07 — Elite Kill / Elite Capture Findings

## Status
RESEARCH / CONTRACT LOCK

## Established

- Elite Kill = verified elimination, neutralization, or closure of a meaningful obstacle, blocker, defect, threat, or failure mode.
- Elite Capture = verified acquisition or establishment of a meaningful capability, qualification, intelligence result, or reusable asset.
- Kill and Capture are independent and may both occur in one verified encounter.
- Both require canonical evidence and verification.
- Failed attempts and recovery remain persistent history.
- Existing verified-event lineage and replay protection remain the accounting boundary.
- Elite outcomes do not automatically create or classify a Boss; Boss classification remains retrospective and Mission Director-governed.
- Only Big Boss ⭐ and Major Boss ⭐⭐ remain valid.
- Outcomes feed verified Points → XP; they do not replace career accounting, qualification, badges, rank, or promotion gates.
- Immersion remains read-only/downstream of canonical state and evidence.
- No Five-Flight changes.

## Boundary
Research/contract lock only. No parallel game engine, automatic Boss detection, new Boss tier, alternate Points/XP authority, or protected architecture change.
