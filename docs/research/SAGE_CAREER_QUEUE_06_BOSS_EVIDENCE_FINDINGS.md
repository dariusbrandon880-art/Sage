## 2. Core Finding — Bosses Are Emergent, Not Pre-Selected

SAGE must not approach ordinary engineering/research work expecting to discover or manufacture Boss Fights.

The organism executes its normal governed workflow:

**REPO TRUTH → RECON → SUPER SEARCH WHEN MATERIAL → BOUND → BUILD → TEST → OBSERVE → REPAIR → VERIFY → EVIDENCE → RECONVERGE → COMPOUND**

A consequential hurdle may emerge during that work. The Mission Director may later identify a completed encounter as a Boss Fight. The Boss classification is therefore a retrospective interpretation of real work, not an upfront task taxonomy.

## 3. Boss → Evidence Relationship

The Boss does not create its own proof authority.

The underlying SAGE organism already produces the evidence needed to reconstruct the encounter through canonical missions/sorties, events, observations, evidence capture, verification, and progression records.

The resulting conceptual flow is:

```text
REAL WORK
    ↓
MISSION / SORTIE
    ↓
EVENTS + OBSERVATIONS
    ↓
FAILURES / RECOVERY / BREAKTHROUGH
    ↓
EVIDENCE CAPTURE
    ↓
VERIFICATION
    ↓
BOSS CLASSIFICATION
    ↓
ELITE KILL / CAPTURE
    ↓
VERIFIED POINTS
    ↓
XP / CAREER CONSEQUENCE
```

## 4. Evidence Must Explain the Fight

When a real encounter is later classified as a Boss Fight, its evidence should be able to answer:

- What was the actual obstacle or objective?
- What capabilities were required?
- What attempts and failures occurred?
- What recovery or adaptation occurred?
- What breakthrough changed the outcome?
- What was actually observed?
- What evidence proves the result?
- What independent verification occurred?
- What capability, qualification, intelligence, or reusable asset was established?
- What was eliminated (Kill) or acquired/established (Capture)?
- What verified Points resulted?

Difficulty should therefore be inferred from demonstrated work and evidence rather than invented before the encounter.

## 5. Classification Timing

A Boss may be identified after the work has produced sufficient history and proof. The classification process should not distort the underlying historical record.

```text
WORK FIRST
   ↓
PROOF ACCUMULATES
   ↓
DIRECTOR DECLARES / CONFIRMS BOSS ENCOUNTER
   ↓
RECONSTRUCT ENCOUNTER FROM EVIDENCE
   ↓
CLASSIFY BIG BOSS ⭐ OR MAJOR BOSS ⭐⭐
   ↓
RECORD OUTCOME
```

Only these two Boss classes remain valid:

- **BIG BOSS ⭐**
- **MAJOR BOSS ⭐⭐**

No additional Boss tiers are introduced by this finding.

## 6. Evidence Is Also the Future Difficulty Ledger

The established Points economy creates a path toward empirical Boss difficulty without requiring arbitrary Boss XP tables.

Future classification can examine the actual verified evidence and resulting Points, including demonstrated difficulty, capability breadth, impact, verification burden, recovery history, and reusable value where those signals exist in canonical state.

The important distinction is:

**Points are evidence-backed accounting of verified performance; they are not permission to invent difficulty.**

Boss difficulty should emerge from the encounter's demonstrated evidence and can become more quantitatively grounded as SAGE accumulates real encounters.

## 7. Failed Attempts Are Part of the Boss Record

A Boss encounter's history must retain meaningful failures, retries, recovery, and breakthroughs. A successful final result must not erase the path required to obtain it.

Repeated work only becomes progression when new verified value is produced. This prevents Boss encounters from becoming farmable labels attached to arbitrary repeated activity.

## 8. Immersion Boundary

Game immersion is one with the SAGE organism at the presentation layer, but it is not a second authority system.

```text
CANONICAL ORGANISM STATE + EVIDENCE
                ↓
       BOSS ENCOUNTER PROJECTION
                ↓
       HUD / NAMEPLATE / CHAT
```

Immersion may express the encounter dramatically, but it must not invent Boss health, damage, kill state, capture state, Points, XP, or verification.

## 9. Big Jump Wave Integration

Queue #06 is not a separate game-development workflow. It travels through the same full Big Jump Wave organism:

**INTAKE & RECON → BOUNDED BUILD → VERIFY & PROOF → WAREHOUSE PROMOTE**

The Boss layer observes and classifies the same work performed by the organism. It does not create a parallel universe of missions, evidence, or progression.

## 10. Protected Boundaries

This finding does not modify, redefine, reassign, or structurally change the Five-Flight system.

It does not authorize a new Boss subsystem, automatic Boss detection, automatic Boss classification, automatic promotion, new Boss tier, fake Boss state, or alternate Points/XP authority.

## 11. Locked Finding

**SAGE does not hunt for Bosses. SAGE builds. Real consequential fights emerge from that work. Once sufficient verified history exists, the Mission Director can declare an encounter a Boss Fight, and SAGE can reconstruct and classify it from the evidence the organism actually produced.**

This makes Boss difficulty an empirical consequence of demonstrated work rather than a fictional difficulty assignment imposed in advance.
