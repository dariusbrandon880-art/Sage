# SAGE Career Queue #08 — Boss Reward Findings

## Status

RESEARCH / CONTRACT LOCK

## Locked Badge Rule

Verified Boss outcomes contribute to durable career badges using the Boss class of the verified encounter:

- **Major Boss ⭐⭐:** every **20** verified Boss kills/captures earns **1 badge**.
- **Big Boss ⭐:** every **30** verified Boss kills/captures earns **1 badge**.

Kills and captures are counted independently. A verified encounter may contribute one kill, one capture, or both when both outcomes are canonically verified.

## Persistence Rules

- Badges are a separate durable accomplishment signal.
- Badges are retained across rank-ups.
- Rank-up board-cycle resets do not erase badge history.
- Existing verified Boss kill/capture history remains canonical evidence.
- Badge accounting must not replace Points, XP, qualifications, rank, or promotion gates.

## Authority Boundary

The rule consumes canonical verified Boss outcomes. It does not create a parallel Boss authority system, fake Boss state, automatic Boss classification, or alternative Points/XP economy.

The canonical progression relationship remains:

```text
REAL WORK
  -> VERIFIED EVIDENCE
  -> BOSS OUTCOME
  -> VERIFIED KILL / CAPTURE
  -> POINTS
  -> CAREER XP
  -> BADGE / CAREER HISTORY
```

Boss classification remains retrospective and Mission Director-governed. Only **Big Boss ⭐** and **Major Boss ⭐⭐** are valid Boss classes.

## Boundary

This Queue #08 lock defines the reward cadence only. It does not introduce automatic promotion, a second career store, Five-Flight changes, fake combat state, or a new authority layer.
