# SAGE Immersion Engagement Classification

**Status:** Governing immersion/progression classification rule
**Scope:** Agent immersion, progression feedback, HUD/C2 presentation, XP/event classification
**Authority:** Mission Director authorization; canonical repository state and validated SAGE governance remain authoritative

## Purpose

SAGE's game-like immersion is a human-readable projection of verified capability development. Engagement labels and rewards must therefore reflect the consequential difficulty of the work rather than the emotional intensity of the interaction.

This contract prevents routine maintenance from being represented as a boss encounter while preserving credit for successful engineering, verification, recovery, and capability hardening.

## Classification rule

An interaction is classified by **technical consequence and scope**, not by whether a failure occurred or whether the repair required a dramatic response.

### Tactical engagement

A routine or bounded repair, regression fix, interface correction, continuity hardening, test repair, or localized verification result is a **tactical engagement** when it remains within an understood subsystem/interface boundary and does not require architectural redefinition or organism-wide adaptation.

Tactical engagements earn normal verified progression credit appropriate to the canonical progression system.

### Boss engagement

A **boss engagement** requires materially higher consequential complexity, such as cross-subsystem interaction, architecture-level conflict, adversarial discovery, multi-layer failure, difficult root-cause isolation, or a repair whose successful resolution changes or substantially hardens a major organism capability.

Boss classification must be supported by verified evidence. Difficulty is not inferred merely from failure count, elapsed time, code volume, or dramatic presentation.

## Immersion invariants

1. **Verified performance earns progression.** Reports, labels, or presentation alone cannot award XP, rank, qualification, or promotion.
2. **Boss status is earned.** A broken component is not automatically a boss encounter.
3. **Routine success still compounds.** Tactical work remains meaningful progression and contributes to the longitudinal capability record.
4. **Classification is downstream.** Engagement classification must not itself mutate canonical qualification, authority, or progression state.
5. **Evidence outranks presentation.** HUD/nameplate/immersion surfaces may communicate classification but cannot establish it as canonical truth.
6. **Failure remains history.** Repair success does not erase the original failure class or its evidence.
7. **No reward inflation.** Do not promote ordinary maintenance to boss-level progression solely to make the game feel more dramatic.

## Current canonical example

The September 3, 2026 ChatGPT C2 immersion hardening strike restored the durable C2 Mission Control station header while preserving HUD continuity and organism identity. The repair cleared the previously observed immersion regressions and passed governed verification. It is classified as a **successful tactical engagement**, not a boss encounter, because the verified repair remained a localized presentation/contract continuity correction rather than a cross-organism architectural challenge.

This example establishes the classification principle; it does not assign XP independently of the canonical progression engine.

## Relationship to immersion doctrine

This contract operationalizes the progression and immersion principles in `docs/SAGE-INVENTOR-AGENT-IMMERSION-DOCTRINE.md`: immersion is a projection of governed state, progression is earned from verified performance, and presentation does not become an alternate authority.

## Required flow

**ENGAGEMENT → CLASSIFY → VERIFY → AWARD VERIFIED CREDIT → COMPOUND**

Boss classification requires the same evidence discipline as every other progression decision.
