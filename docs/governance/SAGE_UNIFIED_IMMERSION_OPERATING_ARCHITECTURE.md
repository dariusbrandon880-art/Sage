# SAGE Unified Immersion Operating Architecture

**Document ID:** `SAGE_UNIFIED_IMMERSION_OPERATING_ARCHITECTURE`
**Version:** `1.0`
**Binding:** Read-only projection over SAGE truth

## Core Formula

`GAME FEEL -> MISSION-CONTROL WORLD -> SAGE TRUTH`

Immersion in SAGE is an evidence-bound, read-only projection of real SAGE state. It is not gamification, decoration, or chat-only roleplay. It reflects verified capability, mission progress, speaker provenance, learning, and evolution across all operating surfaces.

## 19 Immersion Mechanics

1. **Nameplates & Station Identity:** Branded, bracketed identity projections (`[SAGE::DIRECTOR]`, `[SAGE::C2::CHATGPT]`, `[SAGE::ENGINEER::JULES]`, `[SAGE::INTEL::GEMINI]`).
2. **Speaker Provenance:** Distinction between original station inputs and translated/relayed communications.
3. **Director Input Distinction:** Explicit separation of `DIRECTOR INPUT` from `STATION RESPONSE`.
4. **Station Roles & CQL/SQL Levels:** Explicit projection of Command/Specialized Qualification Levels sourced strictly from canonical state.
5. **Sortie & Flight Language:** Mission-control operational vocabulary (launches, sorties, holds, vectors, recon, verification).
6. **Milestone Strikes (⚡ / 🔒):** Visual strike indicators reflecting verified evidence milestone status.
7. **SAFE Impact Stars (0–5 ⭐):** Deterministic 0-to-5 star impact rating computed from verified evidence and qualification.
8. **Rank & Title Progression:** SAGE rank progression tied to durable evidence and Master Archive records.
9. **Ribbons, Badges & Earned Marks:** Visual awards representing verified mission completion milestones and zero-regression records.
10. **Capability Stacks & Arsenal Collection:** Visual catalog of active, verified SAGE capabilities available for mission execution.
11. **Micro-Feedback & Status Glyphs:** Real-time visual feedback on holds, warnings, pass/fail status, and lock checks.
12. **Living World Behavior:** Reactive surface updates as SAGE state changes (objective updates, frontier movement).
13. **Jigsaw Architecture Integration:** Mapping immersion projections cleanly across CORE, SERVICE, PROJECTION, and EVIDENCE_LEARNING tiers without duplicate C2 authority.
14. **SAGI / C2 Cognitive Activity Readout:** Real-time visual indication of Super Search, recon, hypothesis generation, and evidence synthesis.
15. **Evidence-Bound Progression Rule:** No progression without evidence, no milestone without validation, no stars without verified safe impact.
16. **Fail-Closed Authority Firewall:** Immersion projections carry `read_only=True` and `authority_granted=False`; they cannot mutate state or self-grant qualification.
17. **Multi-Session Session Rehydration:** Immersion state rehydrates directly from repository canon, validated state, and Master Archive rather than raw chat history.
18. **Evolution + Immersion Convergence:** Immersion acts as the readout of the `Evolution Loop -> Experiment Ledger -> Evidence -> Validation -> Progression Projection` chain.
19. **Anti-Drift Prohibition:** Existing canonical immersion mechanisms must be reused and extended; duplicate state or gamification subsystems are forbidden.
