# SAGE Internal AI Asset & Compounding Doctrine

**Status:** Strategic doctrine / candidate product-direction record
**Authority:** Mission Director intent + repository implementation truth + validated evidence
**Scope:** Internal development stage; not a commercialization authorization
**Date:** 2026-08-29

## 1. Purpose

SAGE is currently its own primary customer, first tester, and proving ground. The immediate objective is not to rush SAGE to market. The objective is to continuously make SAGE more useful, trustworthy, capable, economical, observable, recoverable, and difficult to replace through real internal use.

Commercial potential is treated as a downstream consequence of demonstrated capability and repeatable value—not as permission to distort the build toward premature packaging.

## 2. Strategic north star

> **Build the AI system that can continuously discover, execute, verify, learn, recover, and compound useful AI capabilities while preserving governance and architectural integrity.**

The target is not a single AI feature or vertical product. Software engineering and enterprise software are the primary internal proving grounds, while reusable capabilities should remain general enough to transfer to other domains when evidence supports the transfer.

## 3. Internal-customer operating model

SAGE's first customer is SAGE itself.

For consequential internal missions, capture:

- the real problem and baseline;
- the SAGE intervention;
- observed outcome;
- human intervention required;
- failures and recovery quality;
- evidence/provenance;
- latency and resource/model cost;
- repeatability;
- capability reuse;
- what became easier or better on the next mission.

This creates a longitudinal product dataset without inventing external customer demand.

## 4. AI capability wealth-engine map

The following are **strategic asset hypotheses**, not claims that every item is already a standalone product or commercially qualified capability:

### A. Governed AI / Jigsaw runtime

Reusable orchestration, bounded authority, execution state, lifecycle control, and architectural placement. The value proposition is a dependable substrate for governed agentic execution rather than a model wrapper.

### B. Deep Recon / context assembly

Repository and external-world reconnaissance that builds decision-relevant context before consequential action. The reusable asset is better context assembly, challenge, and frontier identification.

### C. Big Jump / self-healing execution

The governed loop from recon through build, test, observation, repair, rerun, verification, and reconvergence. The asset is reliable completion of consequential work with bounded recovery rather than simple task generation.

### D. Evidence, provenance, and verification

Machine-readable evidence, exact execution identity, provenance, independent verification, and fail-closed promotion. The asset is trustworthy proof around agentic work.

### E. Runtime economics

Measurement and control of workflow-level resource cost, latency, intervention, reliability, and outcome value. Economics should be optimized at the completed-workflow level rather than token count alone.

### F. Observatory / human control surface

A transparent projection of canonical state, execution, evidence, cost, and exceptions. It must remain a projection—not a competing authority or persistence system.

### G. Experience / capability compounding

Validated outcomes, reusable fixes, negative knowledge, and learning candidates become inputs to harder future missions only after validation. The asset is organizational learning encoded into the system without allowing stale or speculative knowledge to become canon.

### H. Concurrency and coordination

Governed concurrent flight execution, isolation, dependency handling, collision prevention, reconvergence, and future causal coordination. More parallelism is valuable only when evidence quality and decision integrity remain intact.

## 5. What makes an asset valuable

A SAGE capability becomes strategically valuable when it demonstrates a combination of:

**useful outcome + repeatability + low unnecessary intervention + bounded risk + evidence integrity + economic fitness + reuse across missions.**

Architecture size, agent count, test count, autonomy claims, or model count are not sufficient evidence of asset value.

## 6. Continuous compounding loop

**REAL INTERNAL PROBLEM**
→ **BASELINE**
→ **MISSION CHARTER**
→ **SAGI RECON / SUPER SEARCH**
→ **BOUNDED BIG JUMP WAVE**
→ **OBSERVED EFFECT**
→ **FAILURE / RECOVERY**
→ **EVIDENCE + PROVENANCE**
→ **INDEPENDENT VERIFICATION**
→ **ECONOMIC MEASUREMENT**
→ **QUALIFICATION**
→ **REUSABLE CAPABILITY**
→ **HARDER INTERNAL MISSION**

The loop is deliberately internal-first. External commercialization follows demonstrated value rather than leading it.

## 7. Enterprise transfer rule

Software engineering and enterprise software are the initial proving grounds because SAGE can exercise them directly and measure outcomes. Other domains remain potential transfer surfaces.

A capability may be generalized to another domain only after the underlying mechanism, boundary conditions, evidence, and economic value have been validated. A domain analogy is not evidence of transferability.

## 8. Governance boundary

This doctrine does **not** create a second SAGE architecture, authority plane, workflow authority, progression system, or persistence system.

It is a business/strategic lens over the canonical organism.

Authority remains:

1. Mission Director authorization for consequential intent/action;
2. Git/main for implementation truth;
3. validated Master Archive for canonical project knowledge;
4. Research/experimental surfaces for candidate knowledge;
5. external intelligence and relayed agent reports as non-canonical challenge/input;
6. chat memory as continuity aid only.

The Jigsaw architecture remains the placement rule. Existing canonical C2, Big Jump Wave, evidence, observability, learning, and warehouse systems are reused rather than duplicated.

## 10. Commercialization is a later qualification, not the current mission

Do not market a capability merely because it is strategically promising.

Maintain the distinction:

- **ENGINEERING_VERIFIED** — implementation/tests work.
- **EMPIRICALLY_VALIDATED** — governed execution produces the intended observed effect.
- **CLIENT_USEFUL** — the effect materially improves a declared workflow and survives repeatability/economic evaluation.
- **PRODUCTION_QUALIFIED** — operational, security, authorization, observability, recovery, economic, and accountability boundaries are suitable for the declared deployment.

Only validated evidence can advance a capability between these states.

## 11. Immediate operating rule

When choosing the next SAGE frontier, C2 should prefer work that simultaneously:

1. solves a real internal problem;
2. increases reusable capability;
3. improves future mission velocity or reliability;
4. strengthens evidence or governance where needed;
5. produces measurable observed effects;
6. compounds into the next harder mission.

Do not build a commercial wrapper merely because a component appears marketable. **Build the underlying capability until its value is undeniable in SAGE's own operation.**

## 12. Strategic test

Ask after every major mission:

> **Did SAGE become materially more useful, more trustworthy, more economical, more capable, or more reusable—and can the repository prove it?**

If yes, compound it.

If no, investigate the gap.

If unknown, do not pretend it is positive evidence.

**The asset is the compounding system itself.**
