# SAGE 2027 Capability Trajectory

**Locked:** 2026-08-29
**Status:** Strategic operating plan
**Authority:** subordinate to the SAGE Constitution and canonical Jigsaw architecture

## Purpose

Preserve the long-horizon plan discovered through live SAGE operation and external research so future C2 sessions can rehydrate the strategic objective instead of reconstructing it from conversation history.

## North-star thesis

SAGE should not merely accumulate information or complete isolated tasks. It should measurably improve its **way of operating** across missions, while preserving governance, evidence integrity, and architectural coherence.

## Operating trajectory

`GPT -> C2 -> Jigsaw Organism -> Governed Long-Horizon Execution -> Evidence/Verification -> Technique Learning -> Scientific Experimentation -> Validated Self-Improvement`

## Five longitudinal records

Every major mission should contribute evidence toward five measurable records:

1. **Capability growth** — what SAGE can do now that it could not do before.
2. **Execution efficiency** — stalled loops, redundant work, parallel utilization, latency, and resource cost.
3. **Technique improvement** — whether a validated technique outperforms an explicit baseline on later missions.
4. **Reliability** — evidence integrity, false-claim rejection, recovery, reconciliation, and failure containment.
5. **Generalization** — whether an improvement survives across mission classes and conditions.

## Technique-learning doctrine

A successful mission outcome is not automatically a learned technique.

`Mission -> Technique -> Execution -> Observation -> Outcome -> Pattern -> Technique Candidate -> Controlled Validation -> Authorized Promotion -> Reuse`

A technique candidate must remain non-authoritative until replicated, independently evidenced, compared with a baseline, and passed through the existing promotion gate.

## Research frontier inputs

SAGE may investigate principles from:

- advanced computing and distributed systems;
- mathematics, optimization, information theory, causal inference, and formal methods;
- quantum information as an error-aware reasoning analogy and evaluation domain;
- biology as a source of hypotheses about homeostasis, immune rejection, evolution, and adaptive organization;
- scientific computing and reproducible experiment loops;
- HPC and resource-aware scheduling.

These are research inputs, not new SAGE organs or authority surfaces.

## Jigsaw alignment

All future work must remain within the canonical four relationships:

- **CORE** — control, contracts, invariants, constitutional authority.
- **SERVICE** — bounded specialist execution and research capabilities.
- **PROJECTION** — views and interfaces over canonical state.
- **EVIDENCE_LEARNING** — receipts, validation, learning candidates, and promotion evidence.

No fifth relationship or duplicate C2/state/workflow authority is created by this plan.

## Live-customer laboratory

SAGE's development process is itself an empirical laboratory. Real missions provide observations; failures expose missing controls; successful patterns become technique candidates; only validated improvements become durable capability.

The objective is to build a longitudinal record, not to optimize for impressive individual demonstrations.

## 12-month proof target

By approximately August 2027, SAGE should be able to show an evidence-backed trajectory rather than a narrative claim:

- repeated missions across multiple classes;
- baseline-vs-SAGE comparisons;
- measured execution improvements;
- replicated technique candidates;
- adversarial evidence tests;
- recovery/reconciliation evidence;
- documented promotions and rollbacks;
- clear examples where a validated technique improved subsequent missions.

If the evidence does not support improvement, the correct result is to preserve the negative finding and revise the hypothesis.

## C2 session rehydration rule

When a future GPT/C2 session starts, this document should be treated as strategic context. It must not override live repository truth, current mission directives, constitutional authority, or verification evidence.

The session should rehydrate this trajectory, establish exact current state, then execute the highest-leverage authorized frontier rather than repeatedly rediscovering the plan.

> **The goal is not for SAGE to believe it is improving. The goal is to make improvement measurable, reproducible, governed, and cumulative.**
