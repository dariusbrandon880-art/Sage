# SAGE Business ↔ Technical Convergence

**Locked:** 2026-08-29
**Status:** Strategic operating plan
**Authority:** subordinate to the SAGE Constitution and canonical Jigsaw architecture

## Purpose

Preserve the validated strategic convergence between SAGE's technical evolution and its commercial path so future C2 sessions can rehydrate the business objective without resurrecting obsolete historical designs.

## Core thesis

SAGE should become an independent operational-integrity layer for autonomous systems across changing models.

The product thesis is expressed through three durable customer outcomes:

- **History** — trustworthy execution lineage and evidence.
- **Recovery** — recoverable state and resilient continuation after failure.
- **Independence** — customer-controlled operational integrity rather than dependence on a single model/provider.

## Two-door commercial path

### Developer door

A local/developer integration surface that solves an immediate reliability problem with minimal adoption friction.

### Enterprise door

A customer-controlled deployment/sidecar/control-plane surface providing operational integrity, governance, evidence, and auditability at organizational scale.

These are distribution/deployment paths into the same underlying reliability capability, not separate product architectures.

## Trust boundary

SAGE should minimize customer-data exposure by observing structural execution telemetry where possible: state transitions, lineage, hashes, checkpoints, receipts, and other integrity signals. Customer payloads should remain under customer control whenever architecture permits.

Do not claim that this automatically eliminates regulatory obligations. Describe the principle as architectural data minimization and validate compliance requirements per deployment.

## Business ↔ C2 learning loop

Real customer workflows are an empirical laboratory, not merely a sales channel:

`Customer Workflow → Observation → Failure/Need → Technique Candidate → Controlled Validation → Measured Customer Outcome → Authorized Promotion → Reusable Capability`

A technically successful feature is not automatically a business capability. Customer value must be measured where feasible.

## Longitudinal business proof

Track evidence across:

1. **Capability value** — customer workflows newly supported.
2. **Reliability value** — failures detected, contained, recovered, or prevented.
3. **Efficiency value** — reduced rework, latency, stalled execution, or operational cost.
4. **Trust value** — improved lineage, auditability, verification, and evidence quality.
5. **Generalization** — whether value persists across customers, mission classes, models, and environments.

## Historical-plan hygiene

Historical architecture proposals are research records, not automatic requirements. In particular, early infrastructure proposals involving bespoke daemons, filesystem mechanics, custom locks, or specific implementation stacks must not be revived unless current evidence and architecture require them.

The current rule is:

`Preserve validated substrate → reuse existing authority → identify measurable customer problem → implement smallest coherent capability → verify → measure value → promote`

## Strategic alignment with 2027 trajectory

The business plan and technical self-improvement plan are one reinforcing loop:

`Better C2 Technique → Better Mission Execution → Better Customer Outcome → Better Evidence → Better Validated Technique → Better SAGE Capability`

The objective is not to manufacture a narrative of product-market fit. The objective is to accumulate evidence that SAGE produces outcomes customers value and that those outcomes improve as the operating system learns.

## Jigsaw placement

- **CORE:** C2 contracts, constitutional boundaries, product invariants.
- **SERVICE:** developer/enterprise integrations and bounded customer capabilities.
- **PROJECTION:** customer-facing status, lineage, operational views, and reporting.
- **EVIDENCE_LEARNING:** receipts, customer outcome evidence, experiments, technique candidates, validation, promotion.

No business strategy may create a parallel C2, state, workflow, or evidence authority.

## One-year target

By approximately August 2027, aim to possess an evidence-backed commercial trajectory containing:

- real external workflows;
- measurable customer outcomes;
- baseline comparisons;
- repeated reliability/recovery evidence;
- validated technique improvements;
- documented deployment constraints;
- clear developer and/or enterprise demand signals;
- explicit negative findings where assumptions failed.

If evidence contradicts the thesis, revise the thesis rather than protecting it.

> **Build the operating advantage, prove the customer value, preserve the evidence, and let validated results determine the next business move.**
