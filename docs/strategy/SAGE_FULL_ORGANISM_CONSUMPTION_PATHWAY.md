# SAGE Full Organism Consumption Pathway

**Directive ID:** `SAGE_FULL_ORGANISM_CONSUMPTION_PATHWAY`
**Status:** Strategic pathway guide
**Authority:** Subordinate to SAGE Constitution, Master Archive, canonical Jigsaw architecture, and live repository truth.

## Purpose

SAGE must treat consequential concepts, research, failures, business hypotheses, architecture ideas, and external intelligence as inputs to one governed organism—not as isolated notes or chat-local context.

This document is a **pathway guide**, not a second source of truth. Canonical implementation, validated state, evidence, and authorized promotion remain authoritative wherever they already live.

## Core pathway

`DISCOVER → CLASSIFY → LOCATE → CROSS-LINK → RECON → HYPOTHESIZE → EXPERIMENT → ADVERSARIAL VERIFY → VALIDATE → PROMOTE/REJECT → ARCHIVE/COMPOUND → NEXT FRONTIER`

For consequential engineering or repair work, use the stronger repair loop:

`RECON → ROOT CAUSE → REPAIR → REGRESSION → FULL VERIFY → EXACT-SHA RECONCILIATION → PERMANENT LOG → COMPOUND → NEXT ATTACK SURFACE`

## Full Organism Consumption rule

For every consequential new concept, C2 must determine:

1. What real problem or capability does it represent?
2. Which existing organism organ owns it?
3. Is it CORE, SERVICE, PROJECTION, or EVIDENCE_LEARNING?
4. Which existing architecture, business model, research track, or blueprint does it connect to?
5. What prior SAGE work or failed hypothesis must be consulted first?
6. What external evidence could corroborate or falsify it?
7. What measurable experiment would distinguish useful capability from attractive narrative?
8. What evidence is required for validation?
9. What existing promotion boundary would authorize durable adoption?
10. What reusable capability, test, receipt, documentation, or defensive guard remains afterward?
11. What new attack surface or dependency does the change create?
12. What is the highest-value next frontier after the result?

## External intelligence protocol

Super Search / external research is a sensing layer, never canonical authority.

Research should intentionally sample both large and small organizations, startups, open-source projects, researchers, practitioners, technical talks, podcasts/video discussions, postmortems, benchmarks, and credible failures when relevant.

External material is used to:

- discover missing patterns;
- challenge assumptions;
- identify alternative implementations;
- find failure modes;
- compare operational tradeoffs;
- establish baselines;
- falsify SAGE hypotheses.

External claims become SAGE candidate knowledge only after repository-side validation through the existing research → experiment → validation → archive lifecycle.

## Organism alignment map

### CORE
C2, SageRuntime, constitutional contracts, state authority, mission/governance boundaries.

### SERVICE
Super Search, bounded research probes, specialist execution, external intelligence adapters.

### PROJECTION
ChatGPT immersion, Observatory HUD, customer surfaces, reports, progression displays.

### EVIDENCE_LEARNING
Evidence capture, repair logs, Experiment Ledger, Capability Warehouse, technique learning, validated capability records.

No fifth organ category and no duplicate C2, state, or workflow authority may be introduced merely to house a new idea.

## Business + research + architecture convergence

Every mature concept should be cross-linked across three dimensions before promotion:

`CAPABILITY → ARCHITECTURE → ECONOMIC/USER VALUE`

A technically elegant capability without a meaningful problem is a research candidate. A valuable business idea without a validated technical mechanism is a hypothesis. A working implementation without evidence and governance is not durable SAGE capability.

The organism seeks the intersection:

`REAL NEED + TECHNICAL FEASIBILITY + GOVERNED EXECUTION + MEASURABLE VALUE + REUSABILITY`

## Creative frontier handling

Names, metaphors, fictional systems, and conceptual frames—including future SAGE X or other internally named research programs—are treated as **frontier hypotheses** until translated into concrete capability claims.

The process is:

`CONCEPT → IMPLIED CAPABILITY → RESEARCH QUESTION → EXPERIMENT → EVIDENCE → VALIDATED CAPABILITY OR REJECTION`

The metaphor never becomes authority merely because it is compelling.

## Hardening → invention transition

SAGE operates two coupled modes:

**HARDEN:** strengthen governance, reliability, persistence, provenance, recovery, runtime boundaries, and repair intelligence.

**INVENT:** open validated frontiers and investigate capabilities that the hardened substrate now makes possible.

The transition is not a one-time switch. Invention continues while a protected hardening lane attacks newly discovered seams.

Strategic trajectory:

`STABILIZE → COMPOUND → OPEN FRONTIER → INVENT → VERIFY → EVOLVE`

## Historical learning requirement

Before consequential repair or architecture work, inspect prior repair logs, relevant evidence, rejected hypotheses, and previous implementation patterns when available.

After the work, permanently record:

`ROOT CAUSE → REPAIR → TEST → ADVERSARIAL RESULT → INVARIANT → REUSABLE PATTERN → REMAINING GAP`

A repair that is not converted into reusable learning is incomplete from an organism-growth perspective.

## Anti-drift rules

- Do not duplicate existing canonical systems.
- Do not promote research because it is interesting.
- Do not treat external research as repository truth.
- Do not treat presentation as authority.
- Do not allow model output to self-authorize canonical transitions.
- Do not claim capability from narrative completion reports.
- Do not erase negative evidence or rejected hypotheses.
- Do not optimize for feature count, points, badges, or PR count over verified capability.
- Do not stop at implementation; trace the change through evidence, validation, business value, and reuse.

## Success condition

The pathway is working when a fresh C2 session can take a consequential idea and trace it through the organism without reconstructing the reasoning from chat history:

`EXTERNAL/INTERNAL INPUT → CANONICAL LOCATION → RESEARCH → DESIGN → BUILD/EXPERIMENT → VERIFY → VALUE MEASUREMENT → VALIDATION → PROMOTION/REJECTION → PERSISTED LEARNING → NEXT FRONTIER`

> **Consume the idea. Connect it to the organism. Test it against reality. Keep what survives. Make the survivor useful again.**
