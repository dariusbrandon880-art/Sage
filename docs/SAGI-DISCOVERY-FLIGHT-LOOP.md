# SAGI Discovery → Flight Compounding Loop

SAGE's invention surface is governed by a closed empirical loop:

`SUPER SEARCH → DISCOVER → ABSTRACT → COLLIDE → HYPOTHESIZE → FALSIFY → SELECT 5 → FLY 20 CELLS → OBSERVE → VERIFY → UPDATE FRONTIER → DISCOVER AGAIN`

## SAGI role

SAGI is the capability horizon: the system is not qualified by generating ideas,
but by demonstrating that governed discovery produces better experiments,
trustworthy evidence, recovery, reuse, retention, transfer, and compounding.

## Discovery boundary

External research, model output, and cross-domain analogies are candidate inputs.
They are never canonical truth and never authorize execution.

Every candidate carries provenance and is evaluated against the current canonical
frontier and evidence gaps.

## Five-flight portfolio

The default portfolio contains one flight for each role:

1. highest-consequence unresolved frontier;
2. highest expected information gain;
3. strongest falsification challenge;
4. recovery/regression/retention stress;
5. independent transfer or replication.

The roles are coverage requirements, not permanent benchmark identities. A future
selector may substitute a higher-value experiment only with evidence-backed reason.

## Selection invariants

- canonical frontier digest is required;
- every selected candidate has provenance;
- unsafe candidates cannot be selected;
- missing coverage fails closed;
- selection is a proposal, never authorization;
- the flight evaluator remains the authority for empirical verdicts;
- no capability qualification occurs from selection alone.

## Compounding

After the 20-cell wave, observed evidence updates the frontier. New Super Search
then searches against that changed frontier rather than repeating the previous
research question. Selector quality itself becomes observable and can become a
future evaluation target.
