# SAGI-BRAIN-RECON-01

**Record:** SAGE-SAGI-BRAIN-RECON-2026-09-01  
**Authority:** Master Archive / canonical repository truth  
**Status:** ACTIVE RECONCILIATION MISSION

## Objective

Close the SAGI Brain lineage without inventing a replacement architecture. Reconcile the existing SAGI experimental substrate with the historical SAGE research corpus, C2 frontier synthesis, governed Five Flight execution, outcome verification, learning, and Master Archive persistence.

## Canonical lineage

`ORIGIN IDEA → RESEARCH → ARCHITECTURE HYPOTHESIS → EXPERIMENT → IMPLEMENTATION → EVIDENCE → VALIDATION → CAPABILITY → FRONTIER GAP → C2 MISSION → FLIGHT EXECUTION → OUTCOME → LEARNING → VERIFY → ARCHIVE → REUSE`

## Live SAGI substrate

The current `sage/experimental/sagi/` surface contains:

- `state.py` — SAGI state / Ω-state substrate.
- `sagi.py` — bounded candidate generation, temperature and mutation-radius control, failure memory.
- `verifier.py` — candidate verification boundary.
- `controller.py` — atomic evolution cycle, rollback, learning metrics, and SHA-256 evolution receipts.
- `search_loop.py` — search/evolution loop.
- `research_graph.py` — research graph substrate.
- `metacognition.py` — explicit confidence, uncertainty, risk, assumptions, unknowns, and review gating.
- `regret.py` — regret analysis substrate.

These are existing implementation anchors, not proposed replacements.

## Brain layers to reconcile

### 1. State

Canonical SAGI state must remain bounded, integrity-verifiable, and lineage-addressable.

### 2. Discovery

Research graph + search loop must identify candidate knowledge/frontiers from the validated corpus without silently promoting research to truth.

### 3. Candidate generation

Candidate proposals remain bounded by current SAGI state, temperature, and mutation radius. Failed mutations must remain available as negative learning evidence.

### 4. Verification

Candidates must cross explicit verification gates before being treated as successful evolution.

### 5. Metacognition

Confidence, uncertainty, assumptions, unknowns, degradation, and risk must influence review/action gating rather than simulate consciousness or private reasoning.

### 6. Decision learning

Decision autopsy and regret must connect expected decision quality to observed outcomes and produce bounded learning candidates.

### 7. C2 frontier synthesis

Validated SAGI discovery must feed C2 mission synthesis. C2 remains the governance/coordination layer; SAGI does not become an autonomous authority.

### 8. Five Flight execution

C2 converts selected, bounded frontiers into reusable flight missions. Execution remains governed, collision-safe, evidence-producing, and fail-closed.

### 9. Outcome feedback

Execution evidence and verified outcomes return to SAGI as learning signals. No synthetic PASS or unverified outcome may update canonical capability state.

### 10. Archive closure

Validated SAGI learning, lineage, receipts, and capability changes must be persisted into the Master Archive so future sessions do not depend on conversational memory.

## Explicit authority boundaries

- Master Archive = canonical validated knowledge/state.
- Research = candidate knowledge until validation.
- SAGI = discovery, bounded evolution, metacognitive assessment, and learning substrate.
- C2 = mission synthesis, governance, coordination, and dispatch.
- Five Flights = execution vehicles, dynamically assigned rather than permanent roles.
- Validation = independent verification authority.
- No SAGI mechanism may silently promote research, mutate production authority, or bypass governance.

## Closure criteria

SAGI Brain is considered lineage-closed only when:

1. Historical SAGI-related research and architecture concepts are mapped to current artifacts or explicit disposition states.
2. Existing SAGI modules have evidence-backed responsibilities and tests.
3. Research graph/search outputs can be converted into bounded C2 frontier candidates.
4. C2 can dispatch validated candidates through the reusable Five Flight model.
5. Execution evidence can be attributed back to the originating SAGI hypothesis/candidate.
6. Verified outcomes can generate bounded learning inputs for SAGI.
7. Decision autopsy/regret/metacognition are connected without creating a second governance authority.
8. Master Archive records the lineage and evidence required for rehydration.
9. Negative, failed, superseded, dormant, and retired knowledge remains preserved and classified.
10. CI and independent evidence verify the complete chain; production capability is not claimed from tests alone.
