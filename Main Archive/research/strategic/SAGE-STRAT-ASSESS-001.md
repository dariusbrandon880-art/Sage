## 1. Executive Summary & Strategic Position

This record documents SAGE's formal strategic positioning and architectural directives within the broader multi-agent and cognitive governance ecosystem.

### 1.1. Strategic Position
SAGE must be evaluated as an **AI Reliability Infrastructure / Agent Governance Control Layer**.

### 1.2. Operational Directives
To maintain its position as a high-integrity control layer, SAGE strictly enforces the following core attributes:
* **Model Independent:** Operates uniformly above underlying foundation models or enterprise models, ensuring provider and model neutrality.
* **Framework Neutral:** Does not couple to or depend on specific agent orchestration or application development frameworks.
* **Data Minimizing:** Prioritizes localized, zero-trust evidence logging and cryptographic receipts without unnecessary raw data replication or ingestion.
* **Reliability Focused:** Centers on preserving agent events, states, decisions, evidence, failure contexts, and recovery paths.

### 1.3. Hypothesis vs. Validated Fact (Commercial Boundaries)
To ensure absolute engineering rigor and cognitive security, SAGE distinguishes between validated engineering realities and commercial hypotheses.
* **Engineering Validated:** SAGE is fully verified as an AI reliability infrastructure, featuring a robust multi-agent lineage tracker, policy kernel (SPEK), knowledge association layer (SKAL), and runtime integrity layer (SRIL).
* **Commercial Status:** SAGE's commercial validation remains pending.
* **Strict Exclusions:** SAGE must **not** be recorded as:
  * *enterprise proven*
  * *commercially validated*
  * *acquisition candidate*
  * *market success achieved*

These classifications remain hypothetical and require external empirical business evidence to be promoted.
